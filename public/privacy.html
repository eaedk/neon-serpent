Neon Serpent — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Neon Serpent is a single-player game. We respect your privacy: the game does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (scores, coins, unlocked skins, achievements and settings) is saved only on your own device, using the app's local storage. It never leaves your device, and you can erase it at any time under Settings → Reset progress or by uninstalling the app.

No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking

The game has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no third-party trackers. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos, and it works fully offline.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
