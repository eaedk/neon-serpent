## Ratings questionnaires (answers)

- **Apple age rating:** every content question "None" → **4+**.
- **Google Play IARC:** Game; no violence, sex, drugs, gambling, profanity, user-generated content, chat or location sharing → **Everyone / PEGI 3**.
- **Ads:** No. **In-app purchases:** No.
- **Target audience (Play):** 13+ is simplest. Choosing under-13 triggers the Families policy review, which the app would pass (no data collected), but it takes longer.

## Data safety / App privacy

- **Apple App Privacy:** "Data Not Collected".
- **Google Play Data safety:** no data collected, no data shared. Data is not encrypted in transit because nothing is transmitted.
- **Export compliance (Apple):** uses no non-exempt encryption (`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` is already in Info.plist).
